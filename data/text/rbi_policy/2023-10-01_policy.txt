I. Macroeconomic Outlook

trajectory of headline inflation. Energy and food price volatility, geopolitical hostilities, tightening external financial
conditions and climate shocks are the key risks to the outlook. Monetary policy remains focused on aligning inflation
with the target to pave the path for sustained growth in the medium-term.
