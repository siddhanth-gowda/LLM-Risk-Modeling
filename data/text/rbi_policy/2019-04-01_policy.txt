Chapter I Macroeconomic Outlook
I. Macroeconomic Outlook
The macroeconomic setting for the conduct of monetary policy has undergone significant shifts as domestic activity
lost speed in 2018-19 and inflation conditions turned unusually benign under the impact of deflationary food prices.
Going forward, economic activity is expected to recover in 2019-20. Headline CPI inflation is projected to move up
from its recent lows as the favourable base effects dissipate but remain below the target of 4 per cent in 2019-20.
Global economic activity and trade have been shedding momentum and downside risks to the outlook have increased.
