Chapter I Macroeconomic Outlook
I. Macroeconomic Outlook
Amidst heightened trade uncertainties, India’s economic outlook remains resilient, aided by improved consumption,
investment demand and strong macroeconomic fundamentals. GST 2.0 reforms are expected to further boost
domestic demand. Inflation is expected to gradually pick up from Q4:2025-26 on unfavourable base effect,
despite the moderating impact of GST rationalisation. Against the backdrop of volatile global financial markets,
elevated tariff-related risks, and continued geopolitical strife, monetary policy remains focussed on maintaining
price stability and sustained economic growth.
